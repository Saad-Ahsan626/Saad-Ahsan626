## About

I am a Computer Science student at the University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore, focused on end-to-end product development. I build mobile applications in Flutter and design the backend services they depend on, with an emphasis on clean architecture, type safety and maintainable code. I am currently focusing on system design: how to build software that scales, stays reliable under load and remains easy to evolve.
